Triphala Benefits and How to Take It

In this guide
  1. What is triphala?
  2. How people take it
  3. Cautions

What is triphala?

Triphala combines three fruits: amla, haritaki and bibhitaki. It is a classic Ayurvedic digestive tonic.

How people take it

A small spoon of powder in warm water at night is common. Start low, as it can loosen stools.

Cautions

Avoid in pregnancy and diarrhea, and ask a doctor if you take regular medicines.
