Curry Leaves: Benefits for Hair and Health

In this guide
  1. Nutrition
  2. Hair claims
  3. How to use
  4. Conclusion

Curry leaves are a daily kitchen staple with a big hair-care reputation.

Nutrition

They contribute small amounts of iron, calcium and antioxidants when eaten regularly.

Hair claims

Evidence that curry leaves regrow hair is limited. A balanced diet matters more.

How to use

Add fresh leaves to cooking, chutney or buttermilk.

Conclusion

Enjoy them for flavor and nutrition, with realistic hair expectations. Always check with a doctor if you take medicines or have a health condition.
